- Manage opened the paid invoice detail with its receipt action. The modal fit at 375 px with equal client/scroll widths, and Escape closed it.
- Light and dark mobile states were checked. The browser reported no console errors. Fixtures were isolated to the E2E SQLite database; its temporary public image was removed after verification.
- Profile, loading, and upload/security tests: 29 passed, 78 assertions. Invoice overdue tests: 8 passed, 32 assertions. Production asset build and Blade compilation passed. A build initially collided with view-cache regeneration; rerunning after cache generation completed passed.

# Dashboard access, paid renewals, and ticket media review

Mode: after (session override).

Scope: dashboard status entry, lease payment labels, renewal request/review/payment controls, and ticket attachment validation. Existing portal typography, spacing, accent, modal structure, and themes provide the design direction. This is a functional extension of those screens, not a redesign.

## Findings

No remaining antislop findings within the changed controls and copy. Existing unrelated screen styles were not audited or changed.

## Verification

- Application status requires a signed-in tenant and an application owned by that account. Guest redirects, admin rejection, cross-account lookup rejection, and dashboard-only entry are covered by feature tests. Emails direct applicants to their dashboard.
- The renewal form uses a native 1–12 month select and displays the reviewed expiry and full rent. At 375 × 667, the two-month request showed PHP 3,000.00 and January 8, 2027 without horizontal overflow.
- Desktop admin review produced one invoice while retaining the November 8, 2026 lease expiry. Recording full office cash payment moved the expiry to January 8, 2027 and removed the request from the review queue. The lease list visibly showed Paid.
- Tests cover integer-centavo pricing, agreed-rate fallback, calendar month-end dates, rejection, unpaid fulfillment prevention, cash and verified PayMongo payment, duplicate review/payment delivery, repeated renewals, locked terms, and prepaid billing coverage without suppressing earlier rent.
- Both ticket roles share validation before previews and storage. Tests cover PDF/script rejection, mismatched content/extensions, the 10 MB limit, MP4 acceptance, atomic mixed-batch validation, private retrieval, and ownership enforcement.
- Browser upload verification rejected a PDF inline, rejected JPEG content under a PNG extension, and accepted a matching JPEG with a preview and removal control. A test-only upload disk was configured in the isolated E2E router because Livewire uses tmp-for-tests when APP_ENV is testing. Production disk configuration was not changed.
- Full PHPUnit run: 410 of 411 passed. The remaining failure is the existing announcement SMS preview expectation in StallWorkflowUpdatesTest, unrelated to this plan. Focused renewal/access/media/payment checks: 45 passed, 165 assertions. Additional termination/full-term/upload/service checks: 36 passed, 248 assertions. Final renewal/media rerun: 20 passed, 68 assertions.
- The additive migration was applied locally. Blade view compilation and E2E router PHP syntax checks passed. Browser records and payment actions used an isolated SQLite fixture; no live lease payment was recorded.

## Delivery gate

- Hard Gate PASS within scope: the added actions have tested behavior, native selects and file controls have names, errors are inline alerts, and the mobile renewal form fits. No generated assets, security claims, or promotional content were added.
- Purpose-Gate PASS: amount and coverage dates support the payment decision. Paid and Awaiting payment labels communicate actual stored workflow states. Existing portal styling is retained; no decorative techniques or motion were added.
- Liveliness PASS within scope: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1 match the existing transactional portal. The rent amount is the renewal focal point; existing accent and spacing separate term selection, cost, and submission. The established portal controls provide the identity motif.
- Craftsmanship PASS within scope: authorization and ownership are enforced on the server, renewal terms remain fixed until payment, duplicate fulfillment is prevented, unsupported uploads are rejected before previews, and loading/error states were exercised. No unrelated template sections or fabricated claims were introduced.
- Copy checklist PASS: action labels describe the operation, payment requirements are explicit, and the visible lease payment wording is Paid while internal good_standing values remain compatible.
